## Step 4 — Implementation rules

### Code quality
- All magic numbers MUST be named constants in the CONSTANTS block
- No `var` — use `const` and `let` only
- All functions have a single clear responsibility
- Comments explain WHY not WHAT — the code explains what
- No `console.log` left in the final file

### Canvas and rendering
- All visuals rendered with Canvas 2D API primitives (fillRect, arc, beginPath,
  lineTo, etc.) — no external images, no CSS sprites, no SVG files
- Starship: sleek silver/white elongated body, pointed nose, engine bell at rear,
  blue engine glow when thrusting, rendered with path operations
- Background: three-layer parallax starfield (small white dots at varying sizes
  and speeds), seamless horizontal tiling
- Each obstacle type has a visually distinct and recognisable appearance:
  - Debris: jagged grey/dark polygon, irregular edges
  - Satellite: rectangular silver body with extending solar panel rectangles
  - Asteroid: brown/grey irregular circle using multiple arc segments
  - Tesla Roadster: red car silhouette with wheels — recognisable immediately
  - Paperwork Wall: solid grey wall full canvas height with gap — label "NASA
    REGULATIONS" in small text on the wall
- Each collectible visually communicates its purpose:
  - Fuel canister: yellow cylinder shape
  - Starlink: flat white rectangle with small antenna lines
  - Dragon capsule: white rounded cone/capsule with small circular windows
  - X Post: black square with white X logo

### Physics feel
- Gravity should feel weighty — the ship drops noticeably when thrust released
- Thrust should feel punchy — immediate upward acceleration
- The game should feel like piloting a heavy rocket, not a featherweight bird
- Tune GRAVITY and THRUST constants until the feel matches this description

### Audio synthesis
- Use `AudioContext` with `OscillatorNode` and `GainNode` only
- Thruster: low-pitched sawtooth oscillator (~80Hz), volume tied to input state
- Collision: white noise burst via `AudioBuffer` with random values, 0.3s duration
- Pickup: ascending sine wave, 220Hz → 440Hz over 0.15s
- Roadster chord: three sine oscillators at E4/G#4/B4, 0.5s with fast decay
- Booster separation: filtered sweep from 200Hz → 800Hz over 0.8s
- Solar storm: dissonant drone, two oscillators at 60Hz and 63Hz (beat frequency)
- All sounds: wrap in try/catch, fail silently

### Game feel details
- Invincibility flash: alternate sprite opacity between 1.0 and 0.3 every 100ms
- Floating score text: spawns at collectible position, moves up 40px, fades to 0
  opacity, over 1 second — use array of active floating texts
- Screen flash on collision: draw full-canvas red rect at 0.4 opacity for 200ms
- HUD font: `'Courier New', monospace` — fits the mission control aesthetic
- Event messages: centre canvas, yellow `#FFD700`, 20px, fade out over 2 seconds
- Countdown numbers: large (80px), white, scale from 1.5× to 1.0× as they appear

### Entity spawning details
- SOLO pattern: one obstacle at random y within safe zone (top/bottom 60px margins)
- HIGH_LOW pattern: two obstacles, one in top third, one in bottom third
- WAVE pattern: three obstacles at y positions forming a sine-wave vertical pattern
- CORRIDOR pattern: two obstacles with 180px vertical gap between them, player
  threads the needle
- SWARM pattern: four small debris items scattered across full canvas height
- Safe spawn zone for y: between 40px and CANVAS_HEIGHT - 40px
- For patterns with gaps, ensure gap is always navigable (minimum 140px clear)

### Tesla Roadster special treatment
- Spawn it moving slightly faster than other obstacles (1.5× base speed)
- It should drift very slightly up and down (±5px sine wave over time)
- The "Starman" dummy should be visible as a small figure in the driver seat
- Roadster leaves a faint particle trail (small white dots that fade quickly)

### Mars approach transition
- At score 5000: begin overlaying a subtle reddish tint on the background
- At score 6500: distant Mars surface becomes visible at the bottom of the canvas
  (a rust-red curved horizon line with subtle terrain detail)
- At score 8000: the full background is Mars surface — rust sky with darker ground
- The transition should feel earned and visually dramatic
